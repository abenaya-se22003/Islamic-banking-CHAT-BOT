# MAMBU Documentation: Shari'ah Deposit Principles and Process
Source: https://docs.mambu.com/docs/shariah-deposit-principles-and-process

## 1. Overview of Shari'ah Compliance in Mambu
Mambu's core banking platform enables financial institutions to configure and manage Shari'ah-compliant Islamic deposit and financing products alongside conventional products. The engine ensures strict architectural segregation between Islamic and conventional funds, ensuring compliance with AAOIFI (Accounting and Auditing Organization for Islamic Financial Institutions) standards and local Shari'ah supervisory boards.

## 2. Core Shari'ah Principles Supported in Mambu Deposit Products
- Prohibition of Riba (Interest): Standard conventional interest accrual is completely replaced with Shari'ah-compliant Profit Sharing mechanisms.
- Profit and Loss Sharing (Mudarabah Principle): The bank acts as the fund manager (Mudarib) while the depositor acts as the capital provider (Rabb-ul-Mal). Profits earned from compliant investment pools are shared according to a pre-agreed Profit Sharing Ratio (PSR).
- Asset-Backed & Ethical Allocation: Funds deposited into Islamic accounts are mapped strictly to Shari'ah-compliant asset pools and investments (e.g. Murabaha financing, Ijarah leasing), excluding any illicit or non-halal sectors.
- Avoidance of Gharar & Transparency: Pool performance, gross revenue, profit equalisation reserves (PER), and investment risk reserves (IRR) are clearly audited and reported.
- Zakat Support: Capabilities to calculate and deduct mandatory Shari'ah charity / Zakat on behalf of participating account holders where configured.

## 3. The Mambu Profit Sharing Lifecycle and Stages
Mambu manages Islamic deposit returns through a structured 3-stage Profit Sharing lifecycle:

### Stage 1: Profit Calculation
- The core banking engine groups deposits into defined Profit Sharing Pools based on currency, product category, or tenure.
- At the end of the calculation period (e.g., monthly or quarterly), Mambu calculates the total gross profit generated by the pool.
- The system generates a Profit Proposal containing the detailed breakdown of the profit parameters, pool balance, depositor share according to PSR, and bank's management share.

### Stage 2: Profit Approval
- Generated profit proposals enter the "Pending Approval" state.
- Shari'ah officers, operations managers, or automated governance workflows review the proposed distribution and profit rates.
- Once verified against Shari'ah supervisory board rulings and accounting reconciliations, the proposal is transitioned to the "Approved" status.

### Stage 3: Profit Application & Distribution
- Once approved, the profit amounts are applied to individual customer deposit accounts.
- This is executed either automatically during the End-of-Day (EOD) processing cycle or triggered on-demand via the Mambu Profit Sharing REST API.
- The system credits the customer account with the halal profit payout and records the corresponding entries in the mapped General Ledger (GL).

## 4. Pool Accounting and General Ledger (GL) Integration
- General Ledger Mapping: Each Profit Sharing Pool is mapped to dedicated Islamic GL accounts to maintain complete fund segregation from conventional operations.
- Full Audit Trail: Mambu provides immutable logging of all calculation proposals, approval timestamps, and distribution transactions to ensure compliance during internal and external Shari'ah audits.
- Permission Controls: Granular permissions are assigned to users and API consumers for creating, editing, approving, and applying profit sharing proposals.
